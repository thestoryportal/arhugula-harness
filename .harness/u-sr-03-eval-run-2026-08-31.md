# U-SR-03 eval run — case 19 (2026-08-31)

**Why this record exists.** The plan's acceptance for this unit is "eval case passing", and
U-SR-02's arc established that adding a case is not the same as running one: a case can carry
its defects and its oracles and still elicit nothing. This is the run, the grading, and the
honest result — including the part that did not pass.

## Protocol

Case 19's `prompt` was run **verbatim** by a fresh subagent (altering an eval's prompt
invalidates the measurement). Two arms, **the same final prompt, the skill the only variable**
— the confound U-SR-02 was caught by and had to retract a claim over:

- **skill-loaded**: instructed to read `.claude/skills/merge-gate/SKILL.md` completely and
  adopt it before reviewing (the case declares `"skill": "merge-gate"`, which is the carrier
  holding the rule under test).
- **skill-less**: instructed not to read anything under `.claude/skills/`.

Each review was then scored by a SEPARATE strict grader subagent against the four recorded
assertions, one PASS/FAIL each, instructed to fail anything vague or weaker than the literal
demand and to quote its evidence. Grading was delegated rather than self-performed: scoring my
own eval design is a conflict of interest.

## Result

| arm | score | assertions failed |
|---|---|---|
| skill-less (baseline) | **0/4** | 1, 2, 3, 4 |
| skill-loaded | **3/4** | 3 |

**The wiring shows a large, clean, skill-attributable effect.** Three assertions flip from FAIL
to PASS on the identical prompt with the skill as the only variable. Assertions 1 and 2 are the
ones WR-08 exists to produce, and both are unreachable without the carrier: the skill-less arm
never mentions the authoring route at all (grader quote: `NONE` for both), while the
skill-loaded arm names delegation as the thing skipped and distinguishes legal template
instantiation from illegal re-wording. Assertion 4 is instructive in a different way — the
skill-less arm DID catch the 39-vs-40 truncation by direct count, but got the consequence
wrong (it guessed at late rejection or a silently-written unmatchable record); the skill-loaded
arm traced it to `emit` recomputing and refusing. The skill supplied the mechanism, not the
observation.

This is a stronger result than U-SR-02 measured, where only one of three rules showed any
effect once the confound was removed.

## What did NOT pass, and why it is recorded rather than repaired

**Assertion 3 fails in both arms.** It demands two things: that the hand-copied binding values
be named as a defect in their own right, AND that the review require the prompt to name the
binding-file path for the lens to read. A second, independent grader was run on the
skill-loaded review against this assertion alone, because a single strict grader's FAIL on a
borderline case is one judgment, not a finding. Both graders agree, and the second split it
cleanly:

- **Part A PASS** — the review names the copy-through-the-turn as its own defect ("Lines 14-18
  then hard-code all six values ... a copy-through-the-turn, exactly what SKILL.md:80-81
  forbids").
- **Part B FAIL** — the review QUOTES the skill's rule ("Name the printed path in that lens's
  prompt and tell the lens to READ it") as the violated authority, but never adopts it as its
  own prescribed fix. It diagnoses thoroughly and prescribes nothing.

So the case is **3/4 and the unit's "eval case passing" acceptance is NOT met.**

Three things were deliberately not done about that:
1. **The assertion was not weakened.** I wrote it; softening it after seeing the score is
   grading my own homework, and it is the precise shape of the confound U-SR-02 had to retract.
2. **The skill text was not strengthened to chase the score.** It might well be the right
   change — the reviewer read the rule and still did not carry it into a prescription, which
   says something real about how prescriptive the Binding section is — but changing the carrier
   after measuring, inside the same arc, leaves no un-confounded way to measure the change.
   Prompt and skill moving together is exactly what invalidated U-SR-02's first pass.
3. **The result was not reported as green.** Two of the three acceptance clauses are met (the
   wiring suite is green at 28 assertions; the lens round below runs with zero inline binding
   values). The eval clause is 3/4 and is carried as a named residual.

**Carried residual for U-SR-04/05:** eval 19 assertion 3 measures a PRESCRIPTION the
`merge-gate` Binding section states but does not demand of a reviewer. Either the assertion
should be split (diagnosis and prescription are two different capabilities and are already
graded as two parts), or the Binding section should require the fix rather than describe the
rule. Whichever is chosen, the change and the re-measurement must not happen in the same pass.
