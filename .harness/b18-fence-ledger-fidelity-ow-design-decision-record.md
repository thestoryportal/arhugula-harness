## 6. Decision (per question), with the reasoning that binds it

### Q1 — YES: scan at E1, aborted arm FIRST (v1.92 items 1–2 transferred verbatim)
The aborted arm records `completed` terminal-ONLY (no step entry — the fence refused the effect; obligation 3 targets landed effects), NO capture (fact-pin §2 crash-gate: a capture would flip the aborted branch to recovered-terminal and erase the operator's pending ABORT from the crash path). Arm ORDER is load-bearing: an aborted ordinal may also appear in `_recovered_effect_fence_paused` (its re-dispatch was the recovered peer's), so aborted precedes the fence union arm — the FL7-pinned discipline.

### Q2 — YES: union arms over BOTH recovered dicts, `completed` capture-less
The fence half is v1.92 item 3 verbatim. The paused-child half is the honest third-class extension the charter names: a recovered paused-child's attempt-1 dispatched (its child paused mid-flight; its dispatch marker is durable), so `cancelled` is foreclosed by obligation 4; capture-less because the still-journaled prior snapshot carries the peer in `paused_child_branches` — (i) operator stale-resume re-enters the child at its cursor from the snapshot alone, (ii) capture-less keeps the crash gate's child-recoverable classification byte-exact (a `completed` capture would fold the branch terminal and DROP the child snapshot's resumability).

### Q3 — E3 + E4 + E5 + E7 certain; E6 INCLUDED (the one judgment call — flagged for review)
E3/E5/E7 are the direct never-landed M2 analogues (charter names them). E4 is O-W-specific (no parallelization analogue exists to mirror): a FAILED terminal exit that today strands the paused child itself with zero footprint — squarely the defect class. E6 is unreachable from live callers (defence-in-depth guard; both strategy dispatches pass `pause_resumable=True` at 3730 + 3763, HD forwards at 11165) but is an existing TERMINAL exit sharing E7's shape; leaving it scan-free re-creates the class this family closes if any future caller passes `pause_resumable=False`, and the engine function is top-level so a witness drives it directly. Cost = one call line. Include, witnessed. **Pre-build review: CONCUR with INCLUDE** (uniformity per the M2 framing "audit completeness at every TERMINAL fan-out exit"; defence-in-depth framing to be named in the spec delta).

### Q4 — FACTOR into one O-W-local `_synthesize_undispatched_terminals()` closure
The parallelization shape (one discipline carrier, five call sites) is the committed template; seven O-W call sites make inline copies strictly worse (drift surface). E2's inline comments migrate into the closure body; call sites carry the exit-specific one-liners (template: fact-pin 5).

### Q5 — YES: merge E3's two handlers (`except (BranchBarrierDeadlineExceededError, TimeoutError):`)
M2 parity ("handlers merged", v1.91/dashboard record); byte-identical PARTIAL semantics.

### Q6 — fence-this-round keeps its capture at every scanned exit; paused-child capture-less everywhere
E2's capture arm is committed (Codex [P2] lineage: a terminal exit captures no snapshot, so without the store record a crash mis-classifies the peer MAYBE-RAN and re-dispatches into a FAILED round). Carrying the arm unchanged to the new exits keeps ONE uniform arm body; where it newly fires it is a **named crash-visible addition** — the v1.65 §1(c) reproduce-the-terminal trade v1.92 item 4 committed for the sibling. Honest reachability of the capture arm (re-scoped per review findings 2 + 4): **live-reachable at E1** (this-round INERT re-paused peer under a mixed ABORT map — the OW-FL4 analogue) **and E7** (fence-paused round with no protocol bound); **race-reachable at E5** (the finding-2 window, documented per the v1.91 item-3 precedent); **direct-call-only at E6**. E3/E4 structurally never carry class 3. The byte-identical-crash-resume claim is therefore scoped to the capture-less arms (classes 2/4/5/6), exactly as v1.92 scoped it; the fence-this-round capture arm is crash-visible wherever class 3 is reachable.
