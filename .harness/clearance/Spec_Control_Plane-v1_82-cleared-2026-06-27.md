The `_subagent_child_recoverable` child-engine-class conjunct widens from `{EVENT_SOURCED_REPLAY, WAL_SEGMENT, SAVE_POINT_CHECKPOINT}` to `{EVENT_SOURCED_REPLAY, WAL_SEGMENT, SAVE_POINT_CHECKPOINT, RECONCILER_LOOP}` (the dedicated `_SUBAGENT_RECOVERABLE_CHILD_ENGINE_CLASSES` set) — ALL FOUR durable resumable engine classes; `PURE_PATTERN_NO_ENGINE` (non-durable) is the sole non-member. What was reviewed: the grounding that resolved the open at-most-once probe (option (a)). A re-dispatched maybe-ran RECONCILER child runs its OWN crash-resume, which fires the U-CP-97 engine-layer reconverge (`attempt_resume`) gated AT THE CAS REVISION CLAIM, upstream of the driver step-loop (`reconciler_pause_resume_substrate.py` F-1). Three exhaustive cases, all safe: (1) the child never won a claim → the reconverge cleanly RE-CLAIMs → the committed prefix auto-resumes (F2-skipped, not re-fired) → `final_state` reconstructs (CP v1.80 class-agnostic `EngineOutputStore`); (2) a clean `RESUME_CLEAN` → same; (3) the F-1 window — the child WON the claim then crashed mid-re-execution → the retry of the already-claimed revision LOSES → `ABORT_REVALIDATION_FAILED` → the child returns `RunStatus.FAILED` *before any step re-executes* (at-most-once preserved, never a double-fire) → the parent fold raises `SubAgentChildFailedError` (fail-closed; never a SUCCESS aggregate).
