# Clearance — `Spec_Operational_Discipline_v1_38.md v1.38`

v1.38 is the **`B-133`** OD leg — the realization debt v1.37's honest-scope paragraph registered and, correctly, did not repair. It carries **exactly ONE amendment**: a **NEW §C-OD-09 §9.2.1**, *"Realization of the §9.2 floor at the SDK boundary"*, with five normative terms. §9.1, §9.2's nineteen rows and preamble, and §9.3 are **UNTOUCHED and PRESERVED VERBATIM**.

**The sequencing is the point, and it was honoured.** `B-133` demanded the positive control FIRST — *"Grep proves the code path; only the run proves the floor is or is not delivered"* — and closure was conditioned on the RESULT, not on the expectation: had the run shown survival, the row would have closed with that finding and no repair. It showed the drop, at both consumers, for all three members, across seven configurations. Every term below is a consequence of that measurement.

**What the five terms say.** Term 1: the TAIL consumer resolves §9.2 against span EVENT names as well as span names, through the SAME lookup, with event attributes passed so the conditional rows keep conservative-absent. Term 2: a match forwards the CARRIER span immediately — and the coarseness is stated rather than glossed, because OTel has no sub-span export unit. Term 3: the §10.2 keep flag is MIRRORED but the trigger predicate is NOT widened to events — that half is `B-123`'s and is left to it deliberately. Term 4: the HEAD consumer is a **declared bound**, not a discharged one, and the bound is **not vacuous** — `team-binding × local-development` is head-based at a default base-rate of 0.5 with no tail consumer at all. Term 5: an event-matching ROOT close must still materialize its trace decision, so the new arm does not extend the pre-existing `B-136` buffer leak onto the dispatch path where the carrier IS routinely the root.

**Two things this leg found and refused to absorb.** A close-out hypothesis (that head-based-dev cells sample at 1.0 anyway) proved FALSE and is corrected in-text rather than inherited. A pre-existing bookkeeping leak in the name-matching arm was observed and REGISTERED as `B-136` rather than quietly fixed — repairing it would change shipped behaviour for every name-shaped always-sampled root, which this delta has no authority for.

What was reviewed during clearance: the positive control run end-to-end before drafting; the per-cell envelope enumerated by execution rather than read from prose; both consumer call sites, all three emission sites, the tail engagement gate and the module's concurrency posture re-read from the files; the CXA no-delta claim determined by import and signature inspection rather than assumed. Deferrals: none introduced as contract — the cost posture (name-check-first, early exit) and the helper's name and home are implementation discretion, resolved at `Implementation_Plan_Operational_Discipline_v2_33.md` U-OD-59.

Note for Phase 7 consumers: the HEAD half is **NOT** repaired and a green head-bound witness must not be read as a discharge. Terms 1–3 realize the floor **for carriers the head ADMITTED** — and at the production `TAIL_BASED_PROD` cells that is ~10% (`team-binding`) and ~20% (`multi-tenant-compliance`) of event carriers, measured. Of what reaches the tail, the arm delivers 100%. The bound reaches **five of the eight** ACTIVE cells. Closing the emission half requires giving the three members a span shape at emission — a NEW primitive routing through workspace `CLAUDE.md` §4.3 back-flow per X-AL-3. Closing the admission half is `B-137`'s architecture question and is not decided here.
