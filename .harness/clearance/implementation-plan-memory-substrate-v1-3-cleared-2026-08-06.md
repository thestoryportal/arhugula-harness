# Clearance — `Implementation_Plan_Memory_Substrate_v1 v1.3`

v1.3 carries two changes. (1) U-MEM-22's second `Acceptance:` bullet — the plan's only restatement of the C-MEM-19 failure vocabulary — is extended from six values to seven, in place, exactly as the fork prescribes; nothing else in that landed unit is touched. (2) NEW **U-MEM-28** decomposes the impl leg: the seventh enum member, the single declaration flip on the memory-tool input-error type, the stopgap-docstring rewrite that unit's own shipped text promises, the A-ii six-site re-typing in `lifecycle/llm_dispatch.py` with its receiving-type constraints, the fail-fast preservation the spec's retry paragraph requires, the test-fixture re-key, and the witnesses — **eight** of 39 population-table rows flipping, two negative rows with **distinct** literals (one per gate), end-to-end assertions on **both** classify-routed executor paths, a per-site six/two witness, an end-to-end dispatch/retry witness, a per-site fail-fast assertion paired with each of the six internal-site telemetry assertions, and three mutation probes whose discriminating shapes are stated. §3, §4, §4.1, §7 (a new G8 row) and §9 are reconciled by membership or range extension only.

**The one judgement call, recorded so it can be overturned by inspection.** The fork says *"no new unit"*, three times. That prescription is **followed for the half it was written about** — the vocabulary is an acceptance-criterion amendment to U-MEM-22 — and **departed from for the half it predates**: sub-decision A-ii did not exist when that assessment was authored (it was raised at the fork's own review round 4 and scoped only at ratification), and the work it added — a six-site source re-typing, a receiving-type choice, a preserved fail-fast retry disposition, and an end-to-end dispatch/retry witness — has no owning unit. U-MEM-22 is *Implement memory observability*; a dispatch-side re-typing whose load-bearing witness is a **retry** exit classification is neither observability nor inside that unit's dependency set, and folding it into a landed unit's acceptance list would put the work where no impl arc would look for it — the precise failure the ratification's own leg-ownership paragraph exists to prevent. This plan's own convention decides it: each preceding spec leg decomposed its impl leg as one new unit (`B-86` → U-MEM-26 at G6, `B-92` → U-MEM-27 at G7). **Nothing in U-MEM-28 exceeds the ratification's enumerated impl-leg list** — it is a decomposition of it — so a reviewer who prefers the literal prescription can fold U-MEM-28's items into U-MEM-22 without changing a single obligation.

**The seventh re-typing — surfaced and WITHDRAWN** *(raised at out-of-family round 3, mislabelled at round 7, withdrawn as an obligation at round 8)*. `_prepare`'s executor-side exhaustiveness fall-through (`memory_tool_executor.py:373`) raises the input-error type for a harness-internal fault, which spec v1.3's type-boundary rule forbids — and rounds 3–7 had U-MEM-28 requiring it re-typed. **Round 8's objection is correct on this workspace's own rules:** the operator ratified an A-ii list of **exactly six**, all in `lifecycle/llm_dispatch.py`; a spec leg applies operator-decided fixes and does not widen a ratified enumeration on its own reading, and *recording* a contradiction does not authorize it. The obligation is removed and the finding carried instead, with its consequence stated: **at U-MEM-28's close, C-MEM-19's type-boundary invariant has ONE known non-conformant site, recorded and unrepaired** — **owned by its own register row `B-114`**, which outlives `B-88` and carries the grounding pass, the conformance-repair-vs-ratified-extension discriminator, and the repair recipe. The witness arithmetic reverts with it: **eight** population-table rows flip, no executor-side negative row is owed, and **U-MEM-28 contains nothing beyond the ratification's enumerated impl-leg list**.

Also reviewed at clearance: the Class-3 cite-drift sweep the PR #1243 gate routed to this leg. Thirteen `memory_tool_executor.py:NNN` cites in this file drifted when that PR relocated roughly 180 lines of the executor, and **every one sits in text this file preserves verbatim** — the v1.1→v1.2 change-note, or the bodies of the landed units U-MEM-26 and U-MEM-27, whose code cites the v1.2 "Cite hygiene" paragraph already declares historical records of their authoring HEAD. They are therefore corrected **at note level** in the v1.2→v1.3 change-note and rewritten nowhere, which is the convention that governs them. Three of the thirteen are **shape-changed**, not merely shifted — the by-reference retrieval-policy call moved to a different method, the search and promotion scopes now resolve through a helper U-MEM-26 itself introduced, and one of the two `record.envelope.scope` pass-throughs no longer exists — so applying the gate's reported offsets mechanically would have produced three confidently wrong cites. Caveat for Phase 7 consumers: U-MEM-28 is **owed and not landed** at this marker's filing, and its `lifecycle/llm_dispatch.py` offsets are this leg's HEAD, to be re-resolved by content at the impl leg's own.
