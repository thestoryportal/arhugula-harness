# Clearance — `Spec_Operational_Discipline_v1_39.md v1.39`

v1.39 is the **`B-123`** OD leg — the §10.2 half of the span-name-versus-span-event mismatch that v1.38 deliberately left to its own row. It carries **exactly ONE amendment**: **§C-OD-09 §9.2.1 term 3, replaced in full**. Terms 1, 2, 4 and 5, §9.1, §9.2's nineteen rows, §9.3 and the whole of C-OD-10 §10.2 are **UNTOUCHED and PRESERVED VERBATIM**.

**This is a conformance repair, and the distinction is load-bearing.** C-OD-10 §10.2's two event-shaped rows have specified the carrier as an *event* since the contract was written (`Spec_Operational_Discipline_v1_2.md:583-584`, read verbatim). The realization matched by span *name*. The code was non-conformant to cleared contract text — so widening the predicate is not a contestable spec direction and there is no fork to route to the operator. The corollary is that **§10.2 is not amended**: the only text in the corpus that needed to change was v1.38's own explicit decline.

**What v1.38 could not have known.** Term 3's first sentence already required the keep flag to mirror the name path *"if the span is itself a classification trigger"* — but the predicate it deferred to matched by name, and the carrier of an event-shaped trip is named `harness.runtime.retry_breaker_fallback`. The mirror was structurally sound and **vacuous** for exactly the members §9.2.1 exists to deliver. This delta supplies the live case; it does not change the mirror.

**Three things this leg refused to inherit.** (i) A Class-3 rider the register had queued for this delta — that §10.2 row 1's ADR-D2 §1.8 cite does not resolve — was re-grounded and found **FALSE**; §1.8's table has a *"C5 fail-class"* column carrying `permanent-fail` at three rows. The rider is dropped and the register is corrected. (ii) The registration's instinct to cover the `sandbox.violation` sibling rather than assume it fine was honoured by an exhaustive sweep — and the answer is that the sibling **is** fine, so the live scope narrows to the breaker arm while the amendment still covers both names on contract-conformance grounds. (iii) The registration's step-(4) conditional (*"if this probe returns the trigger fires, `B-116`-t3's merits argument changes"*) resolved in the **opposite** direction: the trigger was inert, so that argument is confirmed, not weakened, and needs no carry.

What was reviewed during clearance: the §10.2 table and ADR-D2 §1.8 read verbatim from the files; the predicate body and all three of its consumer call sites read at HEAD; the `add_event(` population enumerated by execution across all seven source trees; the two-roster distinction made a normative term rather than left to implementation; the CXA no-delta claim grounded on the absence of new cross-package consumption. Deferrals: none introduced as contract — the derived constant's name and home, the scan's early exit, and the once-only read of the span's event collection are implementation discretion, resolved at `Implementation_Plan_Operational_Discipline_v2_34.md` U-OD-60.

Note for Phase 7 consumers: this repair is **admission-bounded**. §9.2.1 term 4 is inherited unamended, so the §10.2 sibling tree is preserved only for carriers the head **admitted** — ~10% at base-rate 0.1 and ~20% at 0.2, per `B-137`'s measurement. A green witness must not be read as a full §10.2 floor. §10.2 row 1 (the `validator.fail.permanence` attribute carrier) is outside this family entirely and its inertness remains `B-124`'s.
