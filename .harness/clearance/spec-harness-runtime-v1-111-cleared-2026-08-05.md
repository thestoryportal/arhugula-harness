# Clearance — `Spec Harness Runtime v1.111`

v1.111 carries **THREE amendment sites, all inside §14.8.11**. **(A)** a **NEW §14.8.11.1** — *GC reclaim grace — durable, publication-bounded, elapsed-time first-observation bound* — carrying the council record's **TWELVE** §7.1 conditions as contract terms 1–12, plus the observation record's carrier discipline, an explicit **does-NOT-acquire** list, and the §7.2 tier-conditional counter-anchor. **(B)** a strict **APPEND** to §14.8.11's bounded-retention bullet, cross-referencing §14.8.11.1 and stating that every other term of that bullet is unchanged. **(C)** a strict **APPEND** to the deferred-to-implementation-discretion paragraph, adding the observation record's **file name** and **serialization format** and the emissions' concrete **field keys / message shapes**. **No existing sentence is reworded, retracted or relocated at any of the three sites.**

**The contract delta.** The reclaim rule becomes **CONJUNCTIVE and publication-bounded**: an unacknowledged entry is reclaimed only when its filesystem-timestamp-derived age is past the TTL **AND** the elapsed time since a **DURABLY RECORDED** first observation is past the TTL — **with no third reclaim path**, and with **NO absolute `k × ttl_seconds` ceiling** (form **C-2**). `first_observed_at` is **WALL-CLOCK**, sampled at the **locked, post-re-verification observation point**. The carrier is **(C-i)**: a dedicated **candidate-filename + timestamp** record keyed over **BOTH** sweep classes, published by **temp-write + `fsync` + atomic replace + directory `fsync`** — explicitly **NOT** the write-once no-replace primitive §14.8.11 commits for entries. Record absence reads as **no observation**. Worst-case retention becomes typically `2 × TTL` plus up to two sweep-trigger intervals — and that figure is **CONDITIONAL, never an unconditional bound**: the trigger interval is unbounded in the one-shot process shape (pre-existing, not introduced here) and the figure holds only while the record is present and readable.

Reviewed at clearance: that **Reading A** (retain the sweep-COUNT bound) is not partially adopted and **Reading B** (per-process elapsed time) is **retired explicitly** at term 10, so no impl leg can build the register row's pre-ratification `close_out` verbatim; that the amendment encodes **no** unconditional `N × TTL` claim anywhere, which the ratification explicitly forbids; that §14.8.11 acquires **no** `ttl_seconds` floor, **no** numeric `k`, and **no** hard periodic-sweep requirement (its *"periodic-or-opportunistic"* clause already licenses the shipped trigger shape — verified by direct read, no amendment owed there); and that the **candidate-class** ground is stated as **INDEPENDENCE-OR-NON-INVERTIBILITY** rather than *"the name is already a digest"*, since the digest ground is true of published entries and **false** of temporary-file crash orphans, and a contract grounding the permission on a property only one class has would drive an implementation to refuse the other.
