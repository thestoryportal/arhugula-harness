# Clearance — Spec_Memory_Substrate_v1 v1.2 (B-92 spec leg)

v1.1 → v1.2 discharges the open question v1.1 explicitly carried at its own change-note paragraph **"Named open question carried forward, not discharged."** — whether records captured during a cross-family fallback leg are promotion-eligible under C-MEM-10 — on operator-ratified authority, and adds the one field that makes the ratified answer decidable. Two contracts are amended; the Memory threat model is untouched.

**C-MEM-10** gains a new subsection **"Cross-family-captured promotion candidates"** plus thirteen invariants, carrying RATIFIED **reading B (flag + gate)** in all three of its parts: (a) the *condition* — a candidate is cross-family-captured when its source record carries `captured_cross_family` as `true` (the content was produced on a dispatch whose provider family differed from the record's own partition family) or as `unknown` (never determined, failing closed); (b) the *mark* — such a candidate carries the risk-flag value `cross_family_capture`, a **vocabulary addition and not a schema widening**, since the contract's `risk_flags` is already an open `list<string>` and only the implementation-side enumeration is closed; and (c) the ***gate*** — such a candidate is **review-required and never auto-promotable, under every policy configuration**. The gate is the load-bearing half and is stated in the Contract *and* as an invariant deliberately: the fork's §2 grounding established that no promotion predicate reads `risk_flags` at all, so a mark without an attached obligation lands inert. Eligibility is otherwise **preserved** — reading C's outright refusal is foreclosed, and a cross-family-captured candidate remains promotable through review. Four further obligations were added across out-of-family Codex rounds 1 and 2 and are part of the cleared contract. From round 1: the flag is the **single gate authority**, related to the gate **biconditionally** (never a second parallel derivation), and the mark **must survive to the durable review artifact** — a promotion-written record carries its candidate's risk flags in its own content, so an operator reading the durable `proposed` record can see why it was held. That obligation is discharged in **record content**, not in the C-MEM-08 ledger, whose entry shape is unchanged; the honest consequence is that promotion records written after this amendment carry one additional content key and hash accordingly (forward-shape only — no existing record is rewritten, re-hashed, or moved, and no capture-path content shape is touched). From round 2: the `cross_family_capture` value is **reserved to the deriving writer**, so caller-supplied occurrences carry no authority and are re-derived from the source record in both directions. From round 3, which **reconciled** round 2's carrier answer rather than extending it: the rule is enforced at **two surfaces with different authority** — at *derivation*, the mark is derived once and the gate consumes it (unchanged); at *activation and durable proposal*, a candidate the promotion surface did not itself derive is **not trusted at all**, and the condition is **re-derived from the stored source records** it cites, irrespective of the candidate's own flags and booleans, with an unresolvable source treated as `unknown`. **What the re-derivation withholds is the AUTOMATIC path only** — corrected at round 4, which caught the round-3 wording drifting into outright refusal, i.e. the reading C the operator foreclosed: a re-derived `true` / `unknown` forces review-required, and an activation carrying explicit operator approval still proceeds, because that approval *is* the review this gate routes to. Round 4 extended the same re-derivation to the durable-proposal write, and **round 5 generalized it to every durable write** taken from such a candidate — activation, proposal, and denial alike persist the re-derived mark, so no durable artifact of any status states a provenance the surface did not establish. One consequence is the carrier's whole purpose rather than an edge of it: an operator-approved cross-family-captured record stays **marked** as such, because approval accepts the provenance rather than erasing it. The disagreeing pair is still rejected at every **validating** constructor, but that is now scoped honestly as a *consistency* guarantee — not an unreachability claim, since a non-validating copy can bypass it and no consistency check can see a candidate that simply **omits** the mark. Each round closed a hole its predecessor's fix opened: round 1's single-authority rule left the flag caller-writable and the carrier unbound; round 2's carrier answer could not reach the omitted-mark case and contradicted the arc's own `model_copy` witness. The contract states why the activation re-derivation is **not** the parallel derivation round 1 forbade — that was two derivations at one surface; this is one derivation at each of two trust boundaries.

**C-MEM-03** gains the tri-state field **`MemoryRecordEnvelope.captured_cross_family`** (`true` / `false` / `unknown`), a new subsection stating its derivation rule, its `unknown` semantics, its read-side mapping, its hash-inertness, its forward-only consequence, its multi-writer disposition and its stated bound, plus six invariants. This is the fork's **Q2**, which the filing deliberately left *leveled* and routed to this leg. The pick was made by stating the requirement first: both promotion consumers read **one stored source record** and set **booleans** on the candidate, and the mark's carrier is a payload-free flag vocabulary, so the requirement is **stored-version provenance, present/absent** — not by-family (nothing ratified discriminates between cross-family legs) and not aggregate-run (which would be strictly broader than what was ratified, flagging records the run's own primary produced). On that requirement the fork's `(i-envelope-bool)` is both the cheapest column and the most reliable: the field ships inside the version it describes and cannot go stale relative to that content, where the alternative C-MEM-08 ledger join can match a correctly-qualified row describing a superseded version. `(i-content)` is rejected because content is the hash input — for the content-addressed kinds; the run kind derives `memory_id` from its `run_id` alone (`memory_capture.py:1100-1109`, the branch at `:1106-1108`), a qualification Codex round 1 caught in the first draft's universal phrasing. The envelope is an input to neither derivation on any kind, so the hash-inert conclusion holds universally, verified at HEAD.

**Scope discipline, stated honestly rather than minimized.** Unlike v1.1, v1.2 is **not** pure conformance repair, and it does **not** claim to be: it settles a genuinely open contract question, and it **adds one field**, reversing v1.1's own **"Zero new record type, zero new field, zero new enum member…"** posture one delta later. The change-note says so in those words. The field is optional with `unknown` as its absent value, so the other three envelope construction sites and every pre-amendment serialized envelope still validate under a closed model; it is hash-inert, entering neither `content_hash` nor `memory_id`; and it is forward-only in the precise sense the contract now states — **no determination is ever back-filled**, though a legitimate durable transition (redaction / tombstone / expiry) of a legacy record may materialize an explicit `unknown`, which Codex round 1 established happens by construction and which v1.2 permits rather than prevents. Zero new record type, zero new enum member at the contract, zero change to any ledger, packet, or telemetry shape. One field is added as a **capability** (`captured_cross_family`); three further field lines are **declared** at the C-MEM-10 `PromotionCandidate` block (`source_memory_refs`, `review_required`, `auto_promote_allowed`), which the implementation model has carried since before this delta — declaring existing reality that the surface-2 obligations depend on, adding no capability. C-MEM-01, C-MEM-02, C-MEM-04 through C-MEM-09, C-MEM-11 through C-MEM-13, C-MEM-14 through C-MEM-20, and the whole v1 → v1.1 change-note are preserved verbatim; within the two amended contracts, the C-MEM-03 `SourceRef` / `MemoryScope` shapes, the value-domain-and-derivation subsection and all six existing invariants are byte-unchanged (the `MemoryRecordEnvelope` block gains one line and nothing else), and the C-MEM-10 `### Pipeline` list and three existing invariants are byte-unchanged, while the `PromotionCandidate` block gains **three field lines** (`source_memory_refs`, `review_required`, `auto_promote_allowed`) declaring fields the implementation model has carried since before this delta - a declaration of existing reality that the surface-2 obligations depend on, with no existing field removed, retyped, or reordered. **One caveat the spec states in its own words and this marker repeats:** C-MEM-05's `SemanticRecord` block being byte-unchanged does **not** mean promoted-record content is byte-unchanged — the durable-carrier obligation adds a content key to promotion-written records, which is compatible with the preservation claim only because those declared blocks were never the exhaustive content schema (the path already writes `candidate_id`, `source_memory_refs`, and `review_reason` outside them).

**The tri-state is a contract obligation, not a nicety.** Four conditions yield `unknown`, the first added at round 6 and re-keyed at round 7: the stored content **does not derive from the output of a completed provider dispatch** (a pre-dispatch write, or run-lifecycle metadata — `false` there would assert an equality never tested against a producer). That condition turns on content **origin**, not on which mechanism summarized the content: a harness-summarized summary of a real provider response **is** dispatch-derived and carries a determination, so `summary_source` is explicitly not the discriminator. The other three conditions are derivation inputs that can be absent at HEAD — `provider` is `str | None` at every capture signature; the family authority returns `None` for an unregistered key and its own contract requires scope-boundary callers to fail closed; and the record's own `provider_family` may be the `null` unpartitioned wildcard, against which "cross-family" is undefined. Each records `unknown`, and `unknown` maps where `true` does: review-required, never auto-promotable. That is the B-91 tri-state idiom reused verbatim. Recording `false` for an undetermined comparison is named in the contract as the permissive-by-silence defect the field exists to remove.
