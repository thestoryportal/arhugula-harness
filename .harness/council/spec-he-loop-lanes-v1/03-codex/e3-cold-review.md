## Strongest single finding

C1-01 is the clearance blocker: the reservation protocol calls `rename`/`replace` a CAS while allowing payload writes to the same moving pathname. A perfectly conforming implementation can recreate `open` after `open→merged`, retain contradictory filename/payload states, or overwrite a competing destination. Because reservations are the sole duplicate-scheduling and duplicate-landing authority, that defect invalidates the safety proof for every N, including N=1 crash recovery; the state-transition primitive must be redesigned before an implementation plan can safely decompose the rest of Part A.
