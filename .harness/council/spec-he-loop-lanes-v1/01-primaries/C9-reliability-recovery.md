## Position on spine tensions

**T4 — caller backoff+jitter vs D3 fail-fast lease.** RECONCILE. Splitting the primitive (fail-fast, one attempt) from the caller's retry policy is the architecturally correct reconciliation — a lock-free CAS primitive should never itself sleep while holding nothing. But the split creates an enforcement asymmetry: C-HE-07 exists specifically because the council rejected prose-only ordering for lease-before-merge; §8's "MUST NOT tight-poll" is the same shape of claim with no structural counterpart. Verified at HEAD (F1/F3): a `codex exec`-driven lane (`.agents/skills/two-lane/SKILL.md`) follows `.agents/skills/ship-pr/SKILL.md`, which contains no lease-acquire call, no backoff policy, no reference to `tools/merge_door.py`. Reconciled wording: "§8's caller-retry policy MUST be additionally enforced inside the acquire primitive itself (the primitive refuses more than K acquire attempts per lane_id within a W-second window and logs/escalates on exceedance), not solely by calling-code prose — mirroring C-HE-07's own rejection of prose-only ordering, and covering every carrier (`.claude/` and `.agents/`) that can invoke the primitive."

**T5 — token-compare takeover + E9 re-publish.** RECONCILE. Traced at HEAD (`_claim_arc`/`_claim_owner_is_dead`, `arc_metrics.py:602-638`): mutual exclusion is sound — two concurrent dead-owner takeovers both fall through to a second `publish_exclusive`, still O_EXCL-gated, so exactly one wins regardless of the intervening unlink. The contract's framing ("compare an ownership/generation token") overclaims: it is a liveness predicate (pid+host+`_process_is_alive`), not a monotonic generation counter. Fail-safe in the direction that matters (false "still alive" only stalls reclaim), but pid-reuse false-liveness is a real, uncited residual. Reconciled wording: "C-HE-02 §6 MUST name the mechanism precisely as a liveness-predicate compare (pid+host via `_process_is_alive`), not a generation token, and MUST record OS pid-reuse producing a false 'still-held' verdict as an accepted residual (fails toward stall, not toward duplicate-claim)."

**T6 — OPEN-branch single re-issue vs "never invoked twice".** RECONCILE. The invariant is scoped to the MERGED-ground-truth branch (true by definition). But §5 claims "a request still in flight from the first attempt cannot double-land because the second `gh pr merge` on an already-merged PR fails" — an uncited assumption about GitHub's merge endpoint under two genuinely-concurrent (not sequential) merge calls. Reconciled wording: "C-HE-06 §5 MUST scope the claim as 'never invoked twice once ground truth is observable' and MUST either cite GitHub's documented per-PR merge serialization for the in-flight-concurrent case, or add a `test_merge_door.py` case racing two merge calls against a mocked backend that models non-atomic in-flight processing."

**T2 — loop_status venue + ACTIVATE scoping.** ACCEPT. Verified both defects real at HEAD: `loop_status_path()` (`loop_lib.sh:23-25`) resolves via `hook_project_dir()` (`lib.sh:20-23`) — per-worktree, confirming X6; reducers at `loop_lib.sh:151`, `:177` key `ACTIVATE → delete state` with no `lane_id` scoping. Single-file + shared venue + lane-scoped ACTIVATE is the right fix; `loop_log`'s single `>>` under `PIPE_BUF` is atomic-append-ordered (no read-modify-write anywhere in the append path).
