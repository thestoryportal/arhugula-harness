## §1 — General resume-carrier/topology consistency guard (C-CP-25 §25.15 / C-CP-26 §26.2)

> **The carrier ↔ strategy map.** A `PauseSnapshot` populates EXACTLY ONE topology resume carrier (the §26.2 never-co-set invariant), captured by the pausing strategy and read on resume by ONLY that strategy:
>
> | Resume carrier | Read by (resuming strategy) |
> |---|---|
> | `fan_out_resume` | ORCHESTRATOR_WORKERS, HIERARCHICAL_DELEGATION |
> | `peer_fan_out_resume` | PARALLELIZATION |
> | `handoff_resume` | DECENTRALIZED_HANDOFF |
> | `evaluator_optimizer_resume` | EVALUATOR_OPTIMIZER |
> | `effect_fence_resume` | SINGLE_THREADED_LINEAR (the linear/TOOL_STEP step loop — a fence-ambiguous pause is only ever captured there) |
>
> A plain linear `step_index`-only resume populates NONE of these → never a mismatch (correctly admitted).

> **The guard (resume).** On a resume (`resume_snapshot is not None`), BEFORE any dispatch side-effect, the driver fails closed when the snapshot's populated topology resume carrier is NOT read by the resuming strategy (a topology change between pause and resume): `resume-carrier-topology-mismatch`. Otherwise the existing per-strategy recovery proceeds verbatim. The check fails closed on ANY populated-but-foreign carrier, so a corrupt multi-populated snapshot (the never-co-set invariant violated) also fails closed rather than relying on one carrier happening to match.

> **Subsumes the v1.58 synthesis-only carrier-mismatch.** This guard runs FIRST and keys on carrier-populated alone, so the v1.58 `post-join-synthesis-resume-carrier-mismatch` (which keyed on the synthesis identity, and only on the fan-out carriers) is a strict subset — including its load-bearing both-dropped-synthesis false-pass case (captured `None` == resumed `None`), which the general guard catches on carrier-populated independent of synthesis identity. The synthesis identity material-diff (`post-join-synthesis-resume-material-diff`, v1.58 §1) is RETAINED and now always runs against a carrier-consistent snapshot.

> **Crash-resume path unaffected.** The crash-resume path (`resume_snapshot is None`) RECONSTRUCTS each strategy's own carrier keyed to the executing topology (`_determine_fanout_resume`); a foreign carrier cannot be surfaced there. The guard is scoped to the explicit pause-resume entry.
