## §0 Change-note (v1.113 → v1.114)

### §0.1 The defect, as filed

`[HIGH]` `Spec_Control_Plane_v1_112.md` §1 requires a **REQUIRED, non-downgradable** response-provenance carrier on `ResumeContext`, and §1.3 leaves the **carrier SHAPE to implementation discretion** (*"whether the discrimination is expressed as two frozen sibling types, a discriminated-union field, or an equivalent construction is impl discretion"*). The shipped impl chose sibling types — `AccessorDerivedResumeContext` is a **SUBCLASS** of `ResumeContext` (`harness-cp/src/harness_cp/pause_state_projection.py:437`).

**Pydantic v2 serializes a subclass through a BASE-TYPED field, or through a base `TypeAdapter`, using the BASE's schema.** A round-trip of an accessor-derived context through any `ResumeContext`-typed boundary therefore emits only the legacy field set and **silently DROPS the provenance carrier**. Revalidating yields a plain `ResumeContext`, after which `resume()`'s variant check sees a **legacy** context and `Spec_Harness_Runtime_v1.md` v1.107 §30's staleness precondition **does not fire**.

**This is the SAME residual §1.2 constraint 3 already books** — *"a caller who takes an accessor read and then, independently, constructs a legacy `ResumeContext` is unfenced … the type system cannot track a side effect that happened before construction"* — **but it arrives through an ORDINARY act rather than a deliberate one**, which is the sharper form and the reason the row was filed rather than folded into the existing sentence. The existing residual describes a caller who *chooses* to build the no-read carrier; this one describes a caller who chooses nothing at all, because **the base schema, not the value, decides what is emitted**.

### §0.2 What is amended

**§1.2's residual paragraph gains ONE paragraph.** The appended text (a) states that the ordinary-serialization boundary is **formally OUTSIDE this fence's scope**; (b) NAMES `StepExecutionContext.resume_context` as the ONE live base-typed carrier; (c) RECORDS the three grounded facts that keep the vector unreachable at this leg's HEAD; and (d) states a **normative PROMOTION TRIGGER**.

**No text is retracted — and the one thing this delta DOES decide is stated, not buried.** The existing residual's four sentences — what constraints 1–3 buy, what they do not buy, why closing the deliberate-construction case is declined, and the deprecation route a future arc would have to take — are **PRESERVED VERBATIM** and are not reworded, renumbered, or re-scoped. This delta **APPENDS**. **What it decides is a SCOPE question §1.3(c) left open:** whether *"no construction path produces a legacy variant from an accessor-derived one"* reaches generic base-schema serialization, or only the CP-owned affordances its own detect-then-refuse witness exercises. **This delta applies the narrower reading — which IS the register row's pre-selected disposition (b), verbatim: *"declare the serialization boundary formally OUT of the fence's scope."* The broad reading is recorded in full at §0.5 finding (vi), with its one-step escalation route (disposition (a)) named, so a reviewer can overturn this rather than discover it.**

### §0.3 Why (b)-PLUS rather than plain (b) — and why (a) is NOT applied

`[HIGH]` The row's `close_out` pre-selects **(b)**. Plain (b) — *"declare the serialization boundary formally out of the fence's scope, extending §1.2's stated residual to name it"* — would have been satisfied by a single sentence of general prose. **The row's own NEAREST-APPROACH VECTOR note forecloses that reading:** `ResumeContext` is **ALREADY** a base-typed field of `StepExecutionContext` (`harness-cp/src/harness_cp/workflow_driver_types.py:460`), so the hazard is **one `model_dump` away on an existing carrier**, not a hypothetical future boundary — *"and disposition (b) alone would NOT close that vector."*

**The minimal honest delta is therefore (b) PLUS two additions the row's own note demands:** NAME the carrier, and bind the trigger. A residual that says *"serialization is out of scope"* without naming the live carrier would be **true and useless** — a later arc adding one `model_dump` call to a debug path would land it without ever reading a sentence that applies to it. Naming the carrier is what makes the trigger **checkable at the site** rather than discoverable only by a reviewer who already knows.

**(a) is NOT applied, and the ground is the row's own.** Disposition (a) — a closed variant discriminator on `ResumeContext` itself, making the two variants an explicit discriminated union — is **strictly stronger** and is the ONLY thing that would close the vector at type level. It is declined here because it **breaks the field-set-preserved-verbatim guarantee** `Spec_Control_Plane_v1_112.md` §0.4 makes, would require a byte-compatibility argument for **every existing construction site** of a contract cleared eight versions ago, and the row conditions it precisely: *"(a) is warranted only if a serialization boundary ever appears on a real resume path."* **No such boundary exists at HEAD — verified, not assumed (§0.4).** Applying (a) against a vector that is not reachable would pay the compatibility cost for a harm that cannot occur, and would do it **without the operator gate a field-set change to a cleared carrier owes**.
