## §1 — Amended C-CP-02 §2.5.3 (Layer-3 budget = §3.1 effective, override-resolved)

§2.5.3's two L3-exhaustion paths and the "Timeout = exhaustion" requirement are PRESERVED. The **value** of the enforced bound is amended from the flat `time_budget_ms` to the §3.1 override-resolved effective budget:

> **The Layer-3 router call is bounded by the request's EFFECTIVE L3 `LayerBudget` (C-CP-03 §3.1), resolved against the request's `workload_class` + `persona_tier`.** Resolution precedence per U-CP-06: per-workload-class override → per-persona-tier override → the layer's flat `time_budget_ms` (the default — 200 ms for `LLM_AS_ROUTER` — when no override applies). §3.1 commits this tuning surface explicitly on `llm_as_router` ("the higher-tier persona caps budget tighter on `llm_as_router`"), so a deployment that supplies a per-persona / per-workload L3 override caps the router timeout per that override; a deployment that supplies none enforces the flat 200 ms reservation (byte-identical to the v1.36 behavior). Because **LLM_AS_ROUTER is the terminal layer**, budget exhaustion at Layer 3 (pre-decided OR in-flight over-budget) has no further layer to fall through to — it composes with the §2.5.2 preserved `RoutingCandidateUnresolvedError` raise (timeout = exhaustion, L3 terminal). The **mechanism** (resolve the effective budget from the threaded `budgets` tuple keyed on the request discriminators; wrap the router await with it, `asyncio.wait_for`-style) is impl-discretion; the **requirement** — the router await is timeout-bounded by the §3.1 *effective* L3 budget, and a slow/hanging router is interrupted at it and converted to L3 exhaustion — is spec-binding.

The cheapest-deterministic-first ordering (§2.2) and the per-layer time-budget invariant (§2.4) are unchanged. The §2.5.3 pseudocode gloss `= LayerBudget(LLM_AS_ROUTER).time_budget_ms / 1000` is superseded by the override-resolved value (the flat field is the no-override default).
